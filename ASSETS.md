# Bundled assets

Per CLAUDE.md §13, every bundled asset must be CC0 or similarly permissive, recorded here with
its source and license.

## HDRIs

4 presets the Environment panel lets the artist pick between (plus loading their own
`.hdr`/`.exr` file) — all via [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), all CC0 (public domain),
all downloaded at 1k resolution to keep the repository small. Used by `mc_app`'s 3D viewport
(`crates/mc_app/src/hdri.rs`) as the sky and reflection environment.

- `assets/hdri/studio_small_03_1k.exr` — ["Studio Small 03"](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_03)
  by Greg Zaal. Soft, neutral indoor studio lighting — the default.
- `assets/hdri/venice_sunset_1k.exr` — ["Venice Sunset"](https://polyhaven.com/a/venice_sunset)
  by Greg Zaal. Warm, directional outdoor dusk light.
- `assets/hdri/syferfontein_18d_clear_1k.exr` —
  ["Syferfontein 18d Clear"](https://polyhaven.com/a/syferfontein_18d_clear) by Greg Zaal. Bright,
  high-contrast clear midday sun.
- `assets/hdri/rogland_clear_night_1k.hdr` —
  ["Rogland Clear Night"](https://polyhaven.com/a/rogland_clear_night) by Greg Zaal. Dark,
  low-contrast night sky — downloaded as Radiance `.hdr` rather than `.exr` like the others,
  since Poly Haven's EXR encode of this particular asset is ~4x larger than its `.hdr` one.

## Fonts

- `assets/fonts/MaterialIconsRound-Regular.otf` — "Material Icons" (Round style) by Google, from
  the [material-design-icons](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons) repository.
  License: Apache License 2.0. Used by `mc_app`'s 3D viewport toolbar
  (`crates/mc_app/src/viewport3d_panels.rs`) for the Mesh/Material/Camera/Environment icon
  buttons, via specific glyph codepoints (not the whole icon set, but the font is bundled
  in full since Apache-2.0 doesn't require subsetting).

## Dither

- `assets/dither/blue_noise_64.png` — a 64×64 blue noise mask ("LDR_LLL1_0") from
  [Free blue noise textures](https://github.com/Calinou/free-blue-noise-textures), itself
  generated by Christoph Peters and published on his
  [Moments in Graphics blog](http://momentsingraphics.de/BlueNoise.html). License: CC0 1.0
  Universal. Used by `mc_nodes`'s Dither Pattern node's "Blue Noise" pattern option
  (`crates/mc_nodes/src/dither_pattern.rs`).

## App icon

- `assets/icon/icon_256.png` (window/taskbar icon, embedded via `mc_app::app_icon`) and
  `assets/icon/icon_1024.png` (master, for any future use — app bundle icons, favicon, GitHub
  social image) — the Material Icons Round "texture" glyph (same font/license as above),
  centered on a rounded-rect Neutron Star (`#131718`) background with a soft glass-style
  highlight gradient, rendered in Supernova Light (`#FEE6EA`), per the owner's brand palette.
  Generated with Pillow from the bundled OTF, not drawn by hand.
- `assets/fonts/Geist-Regular.ttf` — "Geist" by Vercel, in collaboration with basement.studio,
  from the [geist-font](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) repository. License: SIL Open Font
  License 1.1. Used as `mc_app`'s UI typeface throughout, replacing egui's default font.
